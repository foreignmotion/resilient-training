# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Adults and families in Middle Tennessee who want practical, hands-on outdoor skills: hikers and campers, parents bringing teenagers, preparedness-minded people, church and community groups. They arrive from word of mouth or the NCRT network, usually on a phone, and want three answers fast: what the class teaches, when it runs, and how to sign up. Parents registering minors need the waiver and guardian steps to be clear and trustworthy.

## Product Purpose
A small marketing and registration site for a not-yet-named Middle Tennessee training organization teaching wilderness survival, medical, ropes, bushcraft, living off the land and homesteading. Today it sells one course (Wilderness Survival Fundamentals, every Wednesday, taught with partner No Compromise Response Team) and collects interest for the rest of the catalog. Success: a visitor can go from home page to a paid, waiver-signed registration in a few minutes, and the owner gets a clean roster with signed waivers stored by version.

## Brand Personality
Capable, plainspoken, field-tested. The voice of an instructor at the trailhead: direct, calm, no hype, no fear-selling. The site should feel like gear that has been used: dark, weathered, legible in low light, with one ember-orange signal color like a headlamp or a fire.

## Anti-references
- Tactical/military "operator" branding: camo, stencil fonts, skulls, aggressive black-and-red.
- Glossy outdoor-retail e-commerce (REI-style product grids, lifestyle stock photography).
- Generic SaaS landing pages: gradient heroes, icon-card grids, hero metrics.
- Cute camp/scouting illustration styles.

## Design Principles
1. **Real field over stock.** Use NCRT's own training photos; never decorative stock. Leave an honest slot until the owner supplies class photos.
2. **Answer, then invite.** Every section states the fact (when, where, cost, what you learn) before the call to action.
3. **Trust in the paperwork.** The waiver, policies and payment flow are where trust is won; make them calm, readable and unambiguous.
4. **Readable at dusk.** Dark ground, high-contrast bone type, generous size, works one-handed on a phone.
5. **Privacy of place.** The training location is never published; the design tells people how they'll get it instead.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA. Real buttons, links and labels; visible 2px ember focus ring with 3px offset; 44px minimum targets; text contrast at least 4.5:1; full keyboard completion of registration; error summaries announced with `role="alert"` and fields marked `aria-invalid`. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Works at 320px wide with no horizontal scroll.
